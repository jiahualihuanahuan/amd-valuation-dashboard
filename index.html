UNVERIFIED: the research brief claimed the capital-gains inclusion rate is 50% up to $250K/yr and 66.67% above it. That federal proposal was reversed and never took effect — verify current rules with a tax professional before using them in any plan.
  • ACB in CAD: in a non-registered account, your adjusted cost base is computed in Canadian dollars using the Bank of Canada exchange rate on each trade's settlement date.
  • Stagger trims: trimming a highly appreciated US stock in one year can push gains into a higher bracket — spreading sales across tax years may help.
  • Withholding tax: AMD pays no dividend today. For US dividend payers, the 15% IRS withholding is exempt inside an RRSP, but not in a TFSA or margin account.
